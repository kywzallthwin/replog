# W2-07: Completed Workout Deletion

State: proposed

## Goal
Allow an owner to permanently delete an erroneous completed workout and immediately remove its effect from history and derived statistics.

## Scope
- Extend the owner-scoped session deletion contract to completed sessions while preserving the distinct active-workout cancel flow.
- Add a destructive action to completed workout detail and a reusable accessible confirmation dialog.
- On success, remove/invalidate workout detail, history, dashboard, progress, and previous-workout caches and navigate to History.
- Ensure derived progress, personal bests, previous references, workout counts, set counts, and volume recalculate from remaining data.
- Add regression coverage for ownership, missing sessions, and cascading session data deletion.

## Out of scope
- Restore, trash, bulk deletion, editing completed workouts, or audit logs.
- Account deletion and retention policy work.

## Acceptance criteria
- Owners can permanently delete their completed workout only after explicit confirmation.
- Cancelling the dialog changes nothing and restores focus.
- A successful deletion removes the workout and its sets from every derived view after navigation or refresh.
- Unauthenticated and non-owner requests cannot reveal or delete the session.
- The destructive flow is clear, keyboard accessible, and usable at 375px.

## Verification
- Focused session API, history/detail, dashboard, and progress tests.
- `npm run check`.
- Manual 375px cancel, confirm, navigation, recalculated PB, and previous-reference checks.

## Risks And Rollback
- Risk: deletion is immediate and irreversible. Require explicit in-app confirmation and exact owner scoping.
- Rollback: application code can be reverted, but deleted user data cannot be restored without a database backup.
- Independent review is required for destructive endpoint behavior before `done`.

## Stop conditions
- Do not add bulk deletion, soft-delete infrastructure, or recovery promises.
