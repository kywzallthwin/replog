## Scope

- Mount the router only after readiness.

Do not edit Worker, Express, root dependencies, `STATUS.md`, or other tickets.

## Implementation

- Wrap `RouterProvider` in `ApiStartupGate`; keep router/auth children unmounted until ready.
- Show `Starting RepLog...` using the branded loader.
- Readiness behavior:
  - Immediate first request.
  - Ten-second request timeout.
  - Five-second retry delay.
  - Ninety-second monotonic total limit.
  - Abortable requests and retry delays.
  - Retry network failures, HTML/invalid JSON, and `502/503/504`.
  - Accept only `{ "ok": true }`.
- Terminal failure shows an accessible Retry action that creates a fresh run.
- Only `401` means unauthenticated. Network, proxy, and server failures show an unavailable state.
- Add a single-flight availability coordinator:
  - Record the last successful API/readiness response.
  - Treat readiness as stale after ten minutes.
  - Preflight stale `POST`, `PUT`, `PATCH`, and `DELETE` before dispatch.
  - After an edge/network failure, let `GET` and `HEAD` wait for readiness and replay once.
  - Mark replays to prevent loops.
  - Never replay a non-idempotent request after dispatch.
- Keep production API resolution at relative `/api` and align `.env.example`.
- Disable login/mutation controls during readiness or submission.
- Preserve operation-specific errors and ensure Retry controls are at least 44x44 at 375px.

## Acceptance Criteria

- RepLog owns the UI throughout a cold start.
- Authentication cannot begin before readiness.
- Outages cannot be mistaken for logout.
- Stale mutations preflight readiness.
- Dispatched mutations are sent once.
- Retry states are accessible and mobile-safe.
